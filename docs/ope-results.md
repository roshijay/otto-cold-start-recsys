# Off-policy evaluation — should we ship co-visitation?

## The business question
"Should we pay the engineering cost to ship the co-visitation recommender instead of the
popularity baseline?" A live A/B test is the gold standard but expensive. Off-policy
evaluation (OPE) estimates the candidate's effect from already-logged data.

## Simulation design (the honest setup)
OTTO's logs were NOT generated by a known randomized policy, so true propensities are
unknowable. We simulate logging policies over our 40,000 test sessions instead. Two attempts:

- **Attempt 1 (alpha=0.0): pure popularity logger.** Softmax over popularity scores (1/5/20
  weights); temperature SOLVED (tau0=895.3) so the top-100 items get exactly 80% of
  impressions -- "mostly popular, occasionally explores."
- **Attempt 2 (alpha=0.5): 50/50 pilot mixture** of the popularity logger and the candidate.
  This is the realistic OPE setting -- pilot logs are where OPE actually gets used -- and
  mixture logging bounds every importance weight by 1/alpha = 2, collapsing variance by design.

- **Candidate (pi_cand):** epsilon-greedy (eps=0.05) over co-visitation top-20, the Phase 4
  winner. (The spec said item2vec; the bake-off changed the ship candidate.)
- **Reward:** 0.1/0.3/1.0 x [shown == held-out aid] (click/cart/order multi-objective).
- **Estimators:** IPS and doubly robust (reward model = global empirical mean reward per item,
  context-free) for BOTH policies off each log; 1000-replicate bootstrap 95% CIs on the lift.

## Results

|                                         | attempt 1 (pure popularity log) | attempt 2 (50/50 pilot log) |
|-----------------------------------------|---------------------------------|-----------------------------|
| informative impressions                 | 2 / 40,000                      | 67 / 40,000                 |
| max importance weight                   | 9,255                           | 2.0 (bounded by design)     |
| V(popularity): IPS / DR                 | 5.0e-06 / 5.2e-06               | 3.04e-05 / 3.00e-05         |
| V(candidate): IPS / DR                  | ~0 / 6.1e-06                    | 3.35e-04 / 3.35e-04         |
| lift, IPS (95% CI)                      | -5.0e-06 ([-1.3e-05, 0])        | +3.04e-04 ([+2.1e-04, +4.0e-04]) |
| lift, DR (95% CI)                       | +0.9e-06 ([-6.6e-06, +6.4e-06]) | +3.05e-04 ([+2.1e-04, +4.0e-04]) |

## Attempt 1: a case study in why OPE needs overlap
A popularity-concentrated logger and long-tail truth almost never coincide (2 matches in
40,000). IPS could only see the candidate through those 2 sessions -- where the candidate
assigns ~zero mass -- and "confidently" concluded the candidate is worse (CI entirely
negative). DR, with a reward model built from the same 2 matches, shrugged: CI overlaps zero.
The lesson: **a confidence interval quantifies sampling noise in your data, not flaws in your
design.** No estimator or CI can report information the log never contained. Attempt 1 is kept
as the diagnostic, not the decision.

## Attempt 2: the evidence clears the bar
With overlap (67 matches) and bounded weights, IPS and DR converge to the same lift:
+3.0e-04 per impression, 95% CI entirely above zero, ~11x the popularity policy's expected
reward (3.35e-04 vs 3.0e-05). The point estimate also matches a back-of-the-envelope check
from Phase 4 recall numbers (0.95/20 x P(a* in top-20) x avg weight ~= 3.1e-04). Two
estimators, different assumptions, same answer -- that is what robust offline evidence looks like.

## Assumptions, stated explicitly
(a) Propensities come from our simulated policies, not OTTO's true logger.
(b) Positivity holds by construction: softmax + epsilon-greedy + mixture keep every
    propensity > 0.
(c) No unobserved confounding *within the simulation* -- but the simulation is not real traffic.

## Limitation
"OTTO logs weren't captured as a bandit experiment, so these propensities are simulated. This
is directional evidence for prioritizing engineering work, not a substitute for a live experiment."

## Recommendation: SHIP -- promote co-visitation to a live experiment
The offline evidence clears the bar for engineering investment: ~11x per-impression reward
lift, significant under both estimators, robust to the estimator choice. The live A/B test
remains the real gate -- offline evidence prioritizes, it does not replace. Next step after
this project: instrument a randomized pilot (the attempt-2 design, for real) and let the
traffic decide.
